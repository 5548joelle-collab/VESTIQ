How to Care for Your VESTIQ Garments (4-Step Guide)

  1. Turn Inside Out: Always turn selvedge denim and heavyweight hoodies inside out before washing to lock in pigment and prevent fading.
  2. Cold Water Wash Only: Machine wash on a gentle cycle at 30°C or lower to prevent organic cotton shrinkage.
  3. Skip the Tumble Dryer: Hang-dry jackets and lay hoodies flat on a drying rack to preserve their boxy drop-shoulder structure.
  4. Steam Instead of Iron: Use a handheld garment steamer on low heat to remove creases without scorching heavyweight cotton fibres.
